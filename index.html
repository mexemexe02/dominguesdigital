What I do

  • Websites

    Built, hosted and maintained. Fast, readable on a phone, and written so a customer knows what you do inside ten seconds. No page builders left half-finished.

  • Booking and attendance systems

    Self-service booking for families or clients, digital check-in, automatic reminders. Set up around your existing routine rather than replacing it.

  • On-site setup

    Door tablets, barcode scanners, kiosk lockdown, printed and laminated scan cards, staff walked through it in person. This is the part that decides whether a system gets used.

  • Google Business Profile

    Profile set up or repaired, categories and service areas done correctly, verification handled, reviews wired into a routine you'll actually keep.

  • Ongoing support

    When the scanner stops reading or a reminder doesn't send, you get a person who knows your setup. Remote for most things, in person when it needs hands.

  • Custom work

    Small internal tools, spreadsheet escapes, integrations between things that don't talk to each other. If it's a bad fit I'll say so rather than build it.

How working together tends to go

A conversation first, usually twenty minutes, where I ask how you currently run things. Not a demo. If what you have already works, I'll tell you that and we're done.

If it's a fit, I set the thing up and sit with you while you use it once. Then you have my number for when something breaks.

On-site work covers Barrie and Simcoe County, and I'll travel into the Greater Toronto Area for a setup day. Remote work goes anywhere in Ontario.
